Questions

Good to know.

What if I don't have an LLC yet?
That's what EKON Hosted is for. You operate under our registered entity, so no LLC or EIN is required — we handle your A2P / 10DLC registration and build the same full CRM and SMS system. Pricing is custom, so book a quick call and we'll walk you through it.
Are there any setup fees?
No. Every plan is $0 setup. You pay your monthly price and that's it — the full build, onboarding, and A2P registration are all included.
What's the difference between the plans?
Every plan includes the full CRM and SMS system. Launch ($97) is the complete platform, no AI. Copilot ($129) adds AI that reads and sorts every reply so you only screen the leads worth your time. Hosted is for operators without an LLC — same build, run under our entity, custom pricing.
How long does A2P registration take?
We handle your A2P 10DLC registration and you're typically live in about 48 hours. Carrier review timelines can vary and aren't guaranteed, but most accounts are delivering quickly.
How does the SMS pricing work?
SMS is pay-as-you-go at $0.0091 per text on every plan — no bundled credits, no overage penalties, no "you ran out on the 22nd" surprises. You only pay for what you actually send.
Does the AI qualify leads on Copilot?
On Copilot, the AI reads every incoming reply and sorts it positive or negative, then notifies you the instant a lead is worth screening — you still run the conversation. Full end-to-end qualifying and automatic appointment booking is coming soon with our AI Qualifier — book a demo to join the early-access list.
Is there a contract? Can I cancel?
No contracts. Every plan is month to month and you can cancel anytime.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Move between Launch, Copilot, and Hosted anytime — no rebuild and no disruption to your data or campaigns.
