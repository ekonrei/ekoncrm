Questions

Good to know.

How much is EKON CRM?
EKON CRM is $125/month with a one-time $350 setup and build fee. There are no long-term contracts.
What does the setup fee cover?
Your $350 setup covers the initial CRM build, pipelines, workflows, campaign setup, custom fields, tags, automations, A2P/10DLC assistance, and 1-on-1 onboarding.
How does the SMS pricing work?
SMS is pay-as-you-go (see rates above) with no bundled SMS credits. Calling is billed per minute as you use it. Carrier and provider throughput and compliance rules still apply.
How long does A2P registration take?
We handle your A2P / 10DLC registration, and approval typically takes around 48 hours. Carrier approval times vary and are not guaranteed.
Does EKON use AI?
EKON currently uses intelligent automation throughout the CRM, including Smart Reply Detection, which helps identify positive and negative seller replies and route them into the right workflow. Our full AI seller setter, EKON AI, is currently being developed and will be available as an optional upgrade in the future.
Is EKON AI included in the $125 plan?
No. EKON AI will be an optional upgrade when it launches. EKON CRM is fully usable without it.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. EKON CRM is month-to-month.
