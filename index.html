FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much is EKON CRM?+
EKON CRM is $125/month plus a one-time $350 setup & build, or $1,250/year plus the same setup. EKON Operator and Operator AI are coming soon — see all plans.
Is EKON just an SMS tool?+
No. SMS + email marketing is where leads start, but EKON runs the whole operation: positive replies, acquisitions, follow-up, contracts, dispositions, buyer blasts, KPIs, and — with Operator — team management.
Do I get multiple workspaces?+
Not by default. EKON CRM runs in one all-in-one workspace — SMS, Acquisitions, Follow Up, and Dispositions together. Adding EKON AI creates 2 connected workspaces: an SMS + AI workspace where your screeners and EKON AI work side-by-side, and an Operations workspace where Acquisitions, Follow Up, and Dispositions stay together. EKON Operator teams run on up to three dedicated department workspaces.
Does AI replace my VA / screener / Acquisitions rep?+
No. EKON AI is built to work alongside your team. It can help sort replies, cover overflow, continue approved conversations, organize seller context, recover stalled leads, and personalize Follow Up. Your people stay in control and can take over whenever needed.
How does EKON AI work with my team?+
Your screeners and EKON AI share the SMS + AI inbox. AI sorts replies, covers overflow, captures seller context, and flags anything that needs a human. Screeners can jump into any conversation at any time, and if a seller asks for a call it can become a handoff right away. Qualified sellers, requested callbacks, and real opportunities move into Operations with the context attached. Offers and contracts stay with your team.
Is this just GoHighLevel?+
GoHighLevel is the engine. EKON is the wholesale system built on top of it — configured pipelines, protected automations, marketing, acquisitions and dispo workflows, and the implementation to run it. You skip the weeks of setup.
What are EKON Operator and Operator AI?+
Operator adds team accountability and reporting — scorecards, speed-to-lead, SLA enforcement, routing, and owner reporting. Operator AI is Operator with EKON AI included. Both are coming soon.
Can I add EKON AI without Operator?+
Yes. EKON AI will be an add-on to EKON CRM (+$197/month + $500 AI setup, or $1,997/year with $0 AI setup). It's in early access and not available for purchase yet.
Can I cancel?+
Monthly plans are month-to-month, subject to our Terms. You keep access through the end of your billing period.
How long does A2P take?+
We submit your A2P 10DLC registration after kickoff. Approval is typically around 48 hours, but carrier timelines vary and are not guaranteed.
Do I own my data?+
Yes. Your contacts, leads, and the data you create are yours, and you can export them anytime.
