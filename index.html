Same workspace. AI layered in.

EKON AI doesn't add another workspace to EKON CRM. It's layered into the one you already have — AI Lead Screener works the Positive Replies, AI Operations Manager works Acquisitions, Follow Up and Dispositions, and AI Complete does both.

  • Positive Replies: EKON AI screens and qualifies positive replies alongside your screeners
  • Positive Replies: screened by your team — the AI Operations Manager starts once a lead reaches Operations
  • Operations: run by your team — no AI in Acquisitions, Follow Up, or Dispositions
  • Operations: AI-assisted — Acquisitions Copilot, Follow Up that remembers the seller, and buyer-conversation assistance
  • One workspace, one login — the CRM still handles negatives, wrong numbers, and STOPs

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is EKON just an SMS tool?+
No. EKON runs the whole wholesale operation — seller marketing, positive replies, Acquisitions, Follow Up, contracts, Dispositions, buyers and KPIs — from one login.
What is included with EKON CRM?+
The complete wholesale CRM, built for you: seller marketing, the Positive Reply system, Acquisitions and Follow Up pipelines, Dispositions with Buyer Blast, a dialer, calendars, KPIs and automations. Onboarding and A2P registration are included.
Is this just GoHighLevel?+
GoHighLevel is the engine underneath. EKON is the wholesale system built on top of it — pipelines, automations and workflows already configured, so you don't have to build anything yourself.
How long does setup / A2P take?+
We build your system after your kickoff call and submit A2P registration for you. Approval typically takes around 48 hours, but carrier timelines vary and aren't guaranteed.
Can I cancel?+
Yes. EKON CRM is month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime. Your data is yours to export.
What is EKON AI?+
Optional AI add-ons, coming soon: the AI Lead Screener, the AI Operations Manager, or AI Complete for both. Your team stays in control of offers, contracts and final decisions. Join early access to hear first.
Can I upgrade as my team grows?+
Yes. Start with EKON CRM, then move to EKON Operator for team management when it launches, and add EKON AI when you're ready.
