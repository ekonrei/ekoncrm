Questions

Affiliate FAQ.

How much do I earn per referral?+
A flat 20% of each new customer's first payment. EKON CRM monthly starts at $475 ($125 + $350 setup), so you earn $95. A yearly signup ($1,600 first payment) earns you $320.
Is it recurring?+
No — you're paid on the first payment only, not on monthly renewals. Every new customer pays out right away.
Do bigger plans pay more?+
Yes. The same 20% applies to every EKON plan, so referrals to EKON Operator pay more as it launches. EKON AI SMS and EKON AI Full are in Early Access with manual onboarding, not automated checkout.
How long does my link track someone?+
90 days. If someone clicks your link and buys within 90 days on the same device and browser, the sale is yours — even if they leave and come back later.
When do I get paid?+
Sales show up in your dashboard automatically. Commissions become payable after a 30-day refund window and are paid out monthly to your PayPal or Wise.
Is there a cap?+
No cap, no quotas, no tiers. Refer one wholesaler or fifty — every paying customer earns you 20%.
How do I join?+
Hit Become an affiliate, create your account, and you'll get your personal link and a live dashboard instantly.
